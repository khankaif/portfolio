## The pain

Zefyron set out to connect the whole innovation ecosystem — startups, investors, corporates and universities — through one AI-driven platform: deal flow, events, pitch decks, investor databases, valuation, and more.

It was an entirely new product category, built from zero while the company was still working out what it was. Without a shared language, each new tool risked looking and behaving differently, and moving between them would feel like switching products.

## Why it mattered

The pitch to users and investors was *one* platform. If the product felt like six unrelated apps, the story fell apart — in the product and in the fundraise.

## What was actually broken

It wasn't the screens. It was that the company was still defining what it was, so every product team was making the foundational decisions again. The real need was a system that could absorb constant change without the product fragmenting.

## The bet

Build the design system before building more UI. Shared tokens, components and interaction patterns first; then every tool is assembled from them, so a new product starts consistent by default instead of being made consistent later.

## Decisions

### Who are we designing for?

Three personas with different goals: founders, investors and corporate partners. I ran stakeholder research and mapped their journeys first, so shared components served all three rather than whichever tool was being built that month.

### How do you keep up with a company that keeps pivoting?

With tokens and patterns rather than finished pages. When direction changed, we changed the system once rather than every screen.

### What does "the product" include?

Everything a user or investor sees. I also owned the Webflow marketing site, the brand, and the pitch decks, so the platform, the website and the fundraise all told the same story.

## Impact

- One design system carried 6+ products, and new tools started from it instead of from scratch
- Faster development cycles, as engineering built from shared components
- Pitch and brand materials I designed were part of the round that raised €1M
